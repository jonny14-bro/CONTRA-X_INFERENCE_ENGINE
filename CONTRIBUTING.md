# Contributing to CONTRA-X_INFERENCE_ENGINE

Contributions, issue reports, research discussion, and reproducibility
feedback are welcome.

## Before contributing

- Open an issue to discuss substantial changes before implementation.
- Do not include private datasets, credentials, model weights, or
  personally identifiable information in issues or pull requests.
- Do not submit third-party material unless you have the right to do so.
- Keep benchmark claims reproducible and disclose relevant limitations.

## Source-code ownership and permissions

The source implementation is proprietary and is not released under an
open-source licence.

Submitting an issue or pull request does not itself grant permission to
incorporate, redistribute, or relicense contributed code. Contribution
terms and any required written permissions must be agreed with the
maintainer before a contribution is accepted or merged.

Research discussion and attribution under CC BY 4.0 apply only to the
materials listed in `RESEARCH-LICENSE-SCOPE.md`. The project brand is
CONTRA-X_INFERENCE_ENGINE; the developer and copyright holder is Jatin Kumar Senapati.

## Review

All contributions are subject to maintainer review. A pull request may be
declined, revised, or held pending clarification of ownership, provenance,
testing, or licensing.
